Our Family

Meet the Frees

We started Free Farmhouse for a number of reasons, but the most important reason was because God told us to move on the idea. When you buy our products, please know that every item has been prayed over and that we serve a God that knows your name and loves you!

As a momma that was trying to find natural alternatives for our family, I started making a lot of our day to day household items and foods at home. We know that not everyone may have the time to do that, so we wanted to bring easier access to these items right here in Haralson County. I left my nursing career to be a stay at home momma to our 3 babies and am so grateful that I am able to do all of this with them. My husband works so hard and helps maintain all of Free Farmhouse while working and showing up for our babies. Our goal in doing this is to not only build a natural lifestyle that we are able to maintain as a family, but also to build knowledge and relationships with y'all. We hope to carry so many more products and eventually meat and animals in the future, but for now, we hope you join us where we have started. We know our products will not fit every person and we have built this business knowing that the right people will find us. Please know we love and appreciate all of your support! Thank you so much!

XOXO, The Frees

About & Values

Rooted in faith, made at home

Free Farmhouse is a small, faith-based, family-run business in Tallapoosa, Georgia, making all-natural skin care and home products.
